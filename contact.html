How to Reach Us

Multiple ways to get to Sir Ambedkar Library.

01 · BY SCOOTER / BIKE
🏍️

Two-Wheeler

The easiest way to reach us. Parking space is available near the library entrance.

02 · BY CAR
🚗

Reachable by car via main roads of Abdullapur. Contact us for parking guidance.

03 · BY AUTO / E-RICKSHAW
🛺
