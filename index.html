Frequently Asked Questions

Scroll to see answers stack up.

01

Is the library really free?

Yes, absolutely. Sir Ambedkar Library is a completely free study space. There are no membership fees, no registration charges, and no hidden costs. Just come and study.

02

What are the library timings?

The library is open daily for around 18 hours. Please check the Contact page or call us for the exact current timings.

03

Do I need to register or book a seat?

No registration is required to study. There are no permanent seats — whenever you come, just find an open spot and sit down. It's first come, first served.

04

What facilities are available?

A quiet, clean study hall with tables, chairs and a disciplined silent environment. Please note we do not have AC, RO water, lockers or Wi-Fi — it's a simple, focused study space.

05

Can I bring my own books and materials?

Yes, absolutely. Bring your own books, notes and study material. Just keep noise minimal and respect other students.
