What We Don't Have — And Why We're Honest About It

We believe in being upfront. Sir Ambedkar Library is a simple, free study space — not a premium facility. We don't have certain things, and we don't pretend to. What we do offer is a quiet, clean place to study — for free.

Air Conditioning Wi-Fi RO Water Lockers CCTV Parking Charging Points Newspapers

If these facilities are important to you, we understand. But if you're looking for a quiet, free place to study with proper tables and chairs — you're welcome here anytime.

The Silent Zone — our signature.

Most libraries say "silence please" and then nothing happens. We actually enforce it.

  • ✓ Strict no-talking policy — enforced consistently, not occasionally.
  • ✓ Phone silent mode required — no calls inside the study hall.
  • ✓ Separate discussion corner — for group study and doubt-solving.
  • ✓ Respectful peer group — other students who also want to focus.
