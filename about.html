About Sir Ambedkar Library

A free space built for focus,
not just studying.

Sir Ambedkar Library in Abdullapur, Meerut is more than a reading room — it is a free, dedicated study environment for students who are serious about their future.

🎉 100% Free — No Membership Fees

Born from a simple idea — students deserve better.

We noticed that many students in Meerut struggle to find a quiet, distraction-free place to study. Home has distractions. Cafes have noise. Public libraries are overcrowded or too far.

So we built Sir Ambedkar Library — a space where focus comes naturally. A clean, silent hall with proper tables and chairs. A strict silence policy. And most importantly, completely free for every student.

Whether you are preparing for UPSC, SSC, NEET, JEE, or any other competitive exam — this is your zone.

📖

Why we are called Sir Ambedkar Library

Our library is named after Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar — a scholar, jurist, social reformer and the chief architect of the Indian Constitution. Dr. Ambedkar believed deeply in education as the most powerful tool for personal and social transformation.

We carry his name as a reminder that knowledge should be accessible to everyone — regardless of background or income. That is why our library is, and will always remain, completely free.
